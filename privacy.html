Privacy Policy

Balanced Technology Solutions (“BTS,” “we,” “us”)
Effective date: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information in connection with our internal QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Time integration tools (the “Service”). The Service is intended for BTS’s own business use and authorized client work, not as a public consumer app store product.

Information we collect

  • Account and authentication data needed to connect to Intuit/QuickBooks (for example OAuth tokens, company/realm identifiers, and related credentials stored securely).
  • Business data you authorize from QuickBooks Online and QuickBooks Time, such as customers, invoices, timesheets, and related bookkeeping records.
  • Operational logs limited to what is needed to run and troubleshoot the Service (for example timestamps and error messages).

How we use information

We use this information only to:

  • Provide timekeeping hygiene, invoicing, reporting, and related bookkeeping workflows for BTS and authorized client engagements;
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the Service;
  • Comply with law and enforce our agreements.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers that help us operate the Service (for example hosting or automation infrastructure), under confidentiality obligations;
  • Intuit/QuickBooks as required to provide the connected features you authorize;
  • Authorities when required by law.

Retention and security

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes above, then delete or de-identify it when practicable. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your choices

You may disconnect QuickBooks access and request deletion of integration credentials by contacting us. Some records may be retained where required for accounting, tax, or legal obligations.
