SMS Privacy Policy

Balanced Technology Solutions (“BTS,” “we,” “us”)
Effective date: September 25, 2026

This SMS Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect mobile phone numbers and related information in connection with our text messaging program (the “SMS Program”). The SMS Program supports outbound operational notifications and related communications for Balanced Technology Solutions, including service and project updates for people who have opted in.

Information we collect for messaging

  • Mobile phone numbers you provide when you opt in to receive texts from BTS.
  • Messaging consent and preferences, including opt-in and opt-out records.
  • Message delivery metadata limited to what is needed to send and troubleshoot messages (for example timestamps and delivery status).

How we use this information

We use mobile numbers and messaging consent only to:

  • Send the SMS Program messages you opted into, such as outbound operational notifications and related BTS follow-ups;
  • Honor opt-out and help requests (for example STOP and HELP);
  • Maintain, secure, and improve the SMS Program;
  • Comply with law and carrier or messaging-platform requirements.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies. You may receive occasional operational texts; volume depends on the work you have opted into. Message and data rates may apply.

Sharing — mobile numbers and messaging consent

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We may share information only as needed with:

  • Service providers that help us operate the SMS Program (for example our messaging platform provider), under confidentiality obligations and solely to deliver the messages you consented to;
  • Authorities when required by law.

Your choices

You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any BTS text. After you opt out, we will stop sending SMS Program messages to that number. Reply HELP for help. You may also contact us using the email below to update or delete messaging preferences where practicable. Some records may be retained where required for legal, compliance, or audit purposes.
